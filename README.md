### 👨🏽‍💻 About Me

I'm a backend developer with **5+ years** building production systems for startups in health, travel, and analytics taking ideas from concept to fully deployed solutions.

I design clean APIs, efficient databases, and maintainable architectures using **Django REST Framework**, **LangChain**, and **GPT-based models** — with a focus on reliability, adaptability, and clarity of design.

Beyond backend, I train and deploy **ML models** (TensorFlow, PyTorch, OpenCV, scikit-learn) for recommendation and personalization systems, and I own the **DevOps** side of shipping them whether on cloud infras like Azure, AWS or Render.

Especially drawn to work that tests both the analytical and creative sides of engineering; precision, reasoning, and a little imagination.
